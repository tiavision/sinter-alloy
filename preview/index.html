sinter-alloy

Opinionated sinter components for what attributes cannot do. Three are built and run live below: drag a row or card by its handle, or focus a handle and use Space and the arrow keys; drag the split's separator. The others are designed; their mockups use the temper attributes they would be built from.

3 built 4 designed

Sortable list

Built <alloy-sortable>

Reorder with a mouse, touch or the keyboard. The row moves at once; a self-saving use then sends the whole order in one request, and a refused one puts the row back.

Board

        Built
        <alloy-board>
      

      
Cards move within a column and between columns; Left and Right move a picked-up card to the
        neighbouring column. A self-saving use sends the target column's order with the card's new group.

      
        

          
          
        

      
      

    


    
    

      

        
Split panes

        Built
        <alloy-split>
      

      
Owns its two panes and keeps their size itself. Drag the separator, or focus it and use the arrow
        keys (Shift for bigger steps); Enter collapses the folders and opens them again; a double-click puts it back. The
        size you leave is there on your next visit.

Date and range pickers

        Designed
        <alloy-range>
      

      
Dates in the page's language, whatever the browser's. A range binds straight to two URL parameters
        as ISO dates. Mockup: a search from 10 to 16 November, in German.

Command palette

        Designed
        <alloy-palette>
      

      
Opens with Ctrl K. It lists the page's own links and shortcut buttons with no
        setup, and asks the server for more as you type. Mockup: “rec” typed in caley.

Long list

        Designed
        <alloy-virtual>
      

      
Renders only the rows in view of a resource paginated with sinter's append mode, and
        loads the next page near the end. Mockup: 2,340 contacts, 100 loaded, 6 rendered.
